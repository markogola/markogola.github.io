Smart TV Remote – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Smart TV Remote ("the app"), made by Odhuno, lets you control your TV from your phone or tablet over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information the app does not collect

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. The developer does not run any server, and does not receive, store or sell any personal information about you.

Information stored only on your device

To reconnect to your TVs without pairing again, the app saves on your device: the names and network or Bluetooth addresses of TVs you add, and the pairing codes or tokens your TVs issue. This data never leaves your device except to talk to the TV it belongs to. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Local network and Bluetooth

The app searches your local Wi-Fi network for TVs and sends remote-control commands directly to them. It uses Bluetooth only to connect to a TV you choose, acting as a keyboard and remote. These connections stay between your device and your TV.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for it before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time with the privacy icon on the app's main screen. You can also reset or limit use of your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them.
