Privacy Policy — Best Locker

App: Best Locker (package com.bestlocker.vault) for Amazon Fire tablets, Fire TV and Android
Effective date: November 20, 2026 · Applies to app version 1.0.1 and later
Contact: markogola82@gmail.com

In short: Best Locker keeps your vault on your device. The developer runs no servers and never receives your files, notes or passcodes. The only data that leaves your device is what the Google AdMob advertising SDK collects to show ads (section 3). We do not sell personal information.

1. Your files stay on your device

Photos, videos, audio, documents and notes you add to Best Locker are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is kept in the Android Keystore. Vault files are stored in the app's private storage and are excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Best Locker has no user accounts and does not upload, sync or share vault contents with the developer or anyone else.

2. Passcodes, biometrics and the activity log

  • Master PIN / pattern / password: stored only as a salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hash in private app storage on your device.
  • Decoy PIN (optional): if you set one, it is stored in private app storage on your device.
  • Fingerprint unlock (optional): handled by the operating system's BiometricPrompt. Best Locker never sees, stores or transmits fingerprint data.
  • Activity log: a list of unlock attempts and vault events kept on your device only. You can clear it in Settings.

3. Advertising — data collected by Google AdMob

Best Locker is free and shows banner and interstitial ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). To deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app may automatically collect and send the following to Google:

Your choices: ads are non-personalized by default. You can turn personalized ads on or off at any time in Best Locker → Settings → “Personalized Ads”. The app checks your device's “Limit Ad Tracking” / “Interest-Based Ads” setting (on Fire OS: Settings → Privacy / Preferences → Interest-Based Ads) before every ad request and always serves non-personalized ads when it is on.

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Best Locker itself does not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos library, camera, microphone, call logs or messages, and does not sell or rent personal information. No other third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs are included.

4. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: only to load ads.
  • Biometric / fingerprint: optional fingerprint unlock on devices that have a sensor.
  • Vibrate: keypad feedback.
  • Files: added only through the system file picker, where you choose each item. Best Locker does not request access to your photo library or storage. When you lock a file, the app may delete the original from shared storage (Android may ask you to confirm) so it is only kept inside the encrypted vault.

5. Optional app disguise

You can choose to change the app's launcher icon and cover screen (for example to a calculator or game) to keep the vault discreet. This only happens when you choose it, and you can switch back to “Best Locker” at any time in the app's Disguise tab.

6. Data retention and deletion

Vault data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete single items, use Settings → “Wipe All Vault Data & Reset App” to erase everything, or uninstall the app (which removes all app data and keys). Because the developer stores no data, we cannot recover forgotten passcodes or deleted files, and we have no copy of your data to provide or delete. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls linked above.

7. Security

Encryption keys are protected by the Android Keystore, vault files are kept in app-private storage, the vault locks automatically when you leave the app, and the vault is hidden from the recent-apps preview on supported devices. No method of storage is 100% secure, so please keep backups of important files.

8. Children

Best Locker is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to opt out of personalized advertising. Since the developer holds no personal data, requests about ad data should be directed to Google; you can always opt out of personalized ads in the app and in your device settings. Contact us with any question.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page with a new effective date, and the in-app policy is updated with each app release.
