Privacy Policy

Published by Odhuno · Effective 1 November 2026

Cube Twister is a 3D puzzle cube game for players aged 5 and up. This policy explains what information the app uses, who receives it and the choices you have. The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information that stays on your device

Cube Twister saves your best time, the number of moves in that solve and whether you have closed the How to play screen. This is stored only in the app's storage on your phone. Odhuno never receives it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

Advertising

Cube Twister is free and is paid for by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The app shows a banner at the bottom of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad between games, and an optional video ad you can choose to watch in exchange for extra hint reveals.

Because children play Cube Twister, every ad request is marked as directed at children, for every player. This means:

  • ads are never personalised and are not based on your interests or on other apps you use;
  • only ads rated suitable for general audiences are shown;
  • the app does not use or send the Android advertising ID.

To deliver these ads, the Google Mobile Ads software in the app collects and sends to Google:

  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out;
  • device and app information such as phone model, Android version, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and tapped;
  • diagnostic information such as crash and performance data.

Google uses this information only to deliver and measure ads, to limit how often you see the same ad and to detect fraud and abuse. Information is encrypted while it is sent. Google's handling of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Odhuno does not sell your information and does not receive personal information about you from Google. AdMob gives us only combined reports, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices about ads

  • No personalised ads. Because ads are never personalised, the app does not ask for consent to personalised advertising.
  • Hint ads are optional. Watching a video ad for extra hints is always the player's choice. The puzzle can be played and solved without it.
  • No ad purchases. Ads do not unlock anything that costs money, and the app has no in-app purchases.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state, so ads can load.
  • Vibration, for a short buzz when you turn the cube and when you solve it.

The app does not use the advertising ID, camera, microphone, contacts, files or GPS location.

Children

Cube Twister is suitable for players aged 5 and up and is designed to be safe for children. It follows the Google Play Families policy and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • no account, chat, social features or links to buy anything;
  • no personal information is collected from children by Odhuno;
  • ads are non-personalised, rated for general audiences, and served by Google AdMob, which takes part in Google's Families Ads Program;
  • the advertising ID is not collected.

Parents or guardians with questions, or who want information about their child removed, can contact us at the address below.

Keeping and deleting information

Information stored on your phone stays there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Information collected by Google is kept and deleted according to Google's own policies. You can manage Google's use of advertising information through your Google account and your phone's ad settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act may give you rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information. Because Odhuno does not hold personal information about you, requests about advertising data are best made to Google. You can also contact us and we will help where we can.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using information in a new way, we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listings on Google Play and the Amazon Appstore.
