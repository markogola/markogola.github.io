Privacy Policy — Kids Drawing Book

App: Kids Drawing Book: Animals & Plants (package com.kidsdrawingbook.app) for Amazon Fire tablets, Fire TV and Android
Developer: Odhuno · Effective date: November 20, 2026 · Applies to app version 1.0 and later
Contact: markogola82@gmail.com

In short: Kids Drawing Book is made for children and collects no personal information at all. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, no in-app purchases and no internet access. Your child's pictures are saved only on the device.

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data — including names, email addresses, contacts, photos, audio, location, device identifiers or advertising IDs. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. The developer runs no servers and receives nothing from the app.

2. Information kept only on the device

WhatWhyWhere it stays
Pictures your child finishes ("My Art")So they can be viewed againThe app's private storage on the device
Sound and music on/off settingTo remember the child's choiceThe app's private storage on the device

Deleting a picture in My Art removes it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in the device settings, removes everything the app has stored.

3. Sharing and saving pictures

A picture leaves the app only if someone using the device chooses to:

  • Share My Art — opens the device's own share menu. The picture is sent only to the app the user picks (for example a messaging or email app), and that app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • To Photos — copies the picture into the device's photo gallery.

We recommend that a parent or guardian helps children when sharing.

4. Advertising, analytics and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party SDKs that collect data. If this ever changes, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires, ask for parental consent.

5. Children's privacy

Kids Drawing Book is directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, it is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR (including their rules for children), the Google Play Families Policy and the Amazon Appstore children's privacy requirements. No parental consent is needed because no personal information is collected.

6. Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. Saving to Photos uses the system's shared photo storage, which on Android 10 and later needs no permission.

7. Security

Pictures are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Nothing is transmitted over a network.

8. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can delete all data the app keeps by removing pictures in My Art, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling it. If you have any question or request, email us and we will reply.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date before the change applies.
